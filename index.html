The problem

Hearing loss is more than "not hearing."

💬

Communication

Following conversations, especially in groups where more than one voice competes for attention.

🏫

Environment

Classrooms, restaurants, meetings, and public spaces each present a different kind of listening challenge.

🔊

Sound

Locating and recognizing sounds around you — not just whether you can hear them at all.

Accessibility should adapt to people — not the other way around.

How HEAR works

From your listening experience to tools you can use today.

🔎

1. Check your experience

A short interactive screening explores how you respond to different sounds and listening conditions.

🧠

2. See your profile

Get a personalized listening profile with the situations that may be more challenging for you.

🎙

3. Use Live Hear

Turn speech into live captions for real conversations, right from your phone or laptop.

HEAR — a web-based accessibility platform for everyday communication. Not a medical device. Does not diagnose hearing loss.
