For the people around you

Make communication more accessible.

Small changes can make conversations easier for everyone.

Before speaking

  • Get the student's attention first
  • Face the student directly
  • Make important information available visually

During class

  • Avoid speaking while facing away from the class
  • Reduce unnecessary background noise
  • Repeat or rephrase important information when needed

Group discussion

  • Encourage one person to speak at a time
  • Identify who is speaking before they begin
  • Provide written instructions alongside verbal ones

At home

  • Get their attention before starting a conversation
  • Keep the TV or music down during conversations
  • Face them when speaking, especially across a room

Family gatherings

  • Choose quieter seating away from kitchens or speakers
  • Let one person speak at a time when possible
  • Check in privately if something was missed, without singling them out

In conversation

  • Face them when you talk, even in passing
  • Rephrase instead of just repeating louder
  • Suggest quieter spots for important conversations

In groups

  • Avoid talking over each other
  • Recap key points if the topic changes quickly
  • Ask what helps rather than assuming

Don't just ask, "How well can you hear?" Ask, "How well can the world communicate with you?"
